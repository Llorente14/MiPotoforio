Popular temples deal with long queues and unclear visit quotas. Devotees arrive without knowing when they can enter, and organizers still track slots and bookings by hand. DarshanEase moves the whole process online.

Devotees browse temples and darshan slots without signing in, filter by temple and date, and book a slot for several people with an optional donation. Payment runs through Midtrans Snap, seats are held until the payment window closes, and a confirmation email arrives with a QR ticket. Bookings can be changed or cancelled later from the booking history.

Seat availability updates in real time over Socket.IO. Organizers manage their own temples and slots, and administrators oversee users, roles and reports from one dashboard. The app is built on the MERN stack: React, Express, Node.js and MongoDB Atlas.
