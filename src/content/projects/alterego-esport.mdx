Alter Ego is my favourite esports team, and its fans have to hop between Linktree, Instagram, Liquipedia, Shopee and Tokopedia to find the roster, results, news or a jersey. This site gathers all of it in one place with the team's own black, red and white identity and its three-faced mask.

The landing page tells the team story and shows the Mobile Legends, Counter-Strike 2 and PUBG Mobile rosters, achievements, partners and news. The store holds 85 products by category, and each product sends buyers to Shopee with Tokopedia as a second option.

All content is validated with Zod at build time, so bad data fails the build instead of rendering a broken page. The 3D logo only loads on desktop, and the site is covered by Vitest and Playwright tests.
